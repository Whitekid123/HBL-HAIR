Before you arrive

Come prepared.

How should my hair be when I arrive?

Wash, blow-dry and removal services are available at the studio if you need them — add them when booking. Please tell us in advance about any scalp sensitivities or special requirements so suitable measures can be taken.

How far in advance can I book?

Appointments are released on the 10th of each month at 12:00am. Saturday slots and Director Stylist times go quickly, so set a reminder.

Can I bring someone with me?

No plus ones are permitted during appointments. The studio is a small space and the chair works best without an audience.

Where do I park?

NCP Northampton St Peters Way, 8 St Peter's Way, NN1 1TP — about a 5-minute walk and free for 1.5 hours. SOL Central Car Park, St Mary's St, NN1 2RL — about 3 minutes. Mayorhold Shoppers' Multi-Storey, Lady's Lane, NN1 2DL / NN1 3AH — about 5 minutes. Please allow enough time for travel and parking.

What if I'm unwell?

Please let us know at least 48 hours before your appointment. Clients displaying flu-like symptoms may be refused service for health and safety reasons. Eligible deposits may be refunded in these circumstances.

What if I'm not happy with my service?

Customer satisfaction matters to us. Please notify us during your appointment or within 48 hours of your visit. Aftercare advice is provided following your service and clients are responsible for following the recommended maintenance guidance. Touch-up services may be offered where appropriate; refunds, redos or correction services are not available after 3 days from the original appointment date.
