Important Booking Information

Availability & Deposits

Appointments are released on the 10th of each month at 12:00am.

  • All deposits are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Punctuality is essential for all appointments.
  • Remaining balances can be paid via cash or card. Exact cash is appreciated where possible.
  • Please allow enough time for travel and parking before your appointment.
  • Pricing varies depending on the stylist level selected. Director Stylist services are priced differently from Graduate Stylist services. Please ensure you are booking with the correct stylist tier before confirming your appointment.

Parking

  • NCP Northampton St Peters Way, 8 St Peter's Way, NN1 1TP — approx. 5-minute walk, free for 1.5 hours.
  • SOL Central Car Park, St Mary's St, NN1 2RL — approx. 3-minute walk.
  • Mayorhold Shoppers' Multi-Storey, Lady's Lane, NN1 2DL / NN1 3AH — approx. 5-minute walk.

Late Policy & No-Shows

  • Clients arriving 20 minutes late without prior notice will automatically have their appointment cancelled and marked as a NO-SHOW.
  • Emergency appointments are subject to an additional £50 fee.
  • Repeated NO-SHOW appointments may result in booking restrictions.
  • No plus ones are permitted during appointments.

Appointment Preparation

  • Wash, blow-dry and removal services are available.
  • Please inform us in advance of any scalp sensitivities or special requirements so suitable measures can be taken.

Colouring Services

For colouring, dye or highlight enquiries, please contact us on 07479 412516 before booking.

Colour services are limited to natural tones and start from £20. Available shades include:

  • Black
  • Honey Brown / Brown
  • Other primary colours

Salon Policies

HAIRBYLAURAN LTD reserves the right to refuse service in cases of inappropriate behaviour. Any incidents will be documented and reported where necessary.

  • Smoking or vaping of any kind is strictly not allowed.
  • The business is fully insured.
  • If you are feeling unwell, please notify us at least 48 hours before your appointment. Clients displaying flu-like symptoms may be refused service for health and safety reasons. Eligible deposits may be refunded in these circumstances.
  • All prices listed are starting prices and may increase depending on hair length or service requirements.
  • Please ensure your booking details are entered correctly, as we are not responsible for missed notifications due to incorrect information provided.
  • Any personal belongings left behind for more than 7 days may be disposed of.
  • For appointment enquiries, please contact us via Instagram or WhatsApp (message only).

Customer Satisfaction

Customer satisfaction and quality service are extremely important to us. If you are unhappy with your service, please notify us during your appointment or within 48 hours of your visit.

Aftercare advice will be provided following your service, and clients are responsible for following the recommended maintenance guidance. Touch-up services may be offered where appropriate; however, refunds, redos or correction services are not available after 3 days from the original appointment date.

Thank you for choosing HAIRBYLAURAN LTD — we look forward to welcoming you soon.
