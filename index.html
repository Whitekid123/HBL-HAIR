The Studio

A chair that
isn't rushed.

HBL is a small appointment-led studio on College Street Mews, built around textured hair and locs in particular. One client in the chair, the time your hair actually needs, and honest advice about what will still look good in six weeks.

Whether you're starting your loc journey, keeping a mature set tidy, or want a protective style that survives the working week — you'll leave with something clean, comfortable, and worth photographing.

  • Scalp-first approach — no painful tension
  • Director and Graduate stylist tiers, priced accordingly
  • Aftercare guidance provided with every service
  • Fully insured business

How it works

Four steps
to the chair.

01

Appointments release

Slots open on the 10th of each month at 12:00am. Popular Saturday times go first.

02

Pick your stylist tier

Director Stylist (Laura) or Graduate Stylist. Pricing differs — make sure you book the right tier.

03

Secure with a deposit

Deposits are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable. The balance is paid on the day by cash or card.

04

Arrive prepared

Allow time for parking. Tell us in advance about scalp sensitivities. Punctuality is essential.
