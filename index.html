The studio

A calm chair, an unhurried finish.

FAQ

Good to know

How should I come in for my appointment?

Clean, fully dried and detangled hair unless you've booked a wash with your service. For a retwist, come with roots free of heavy product build-up.

Do you take walk-ins?

Where the diary allows, yes — but appointments always take priority. Message ahead if you can, especially for anything over two hours.

Do I need to bring my own hair?

For braids and feed-ins, bring your preferred brand and colour if you have one. If not, tell us in advance and we'll source it for you.

How long will my style last?

Twists sit well for 2–3 weeks, braids 6–8 weeks, and a retwist typically runs 4–6 weeks depending on your hair and how you wrap it at night.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Repeated no-shows may need a deposit to rebook.
